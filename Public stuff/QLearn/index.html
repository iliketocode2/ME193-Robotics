Settings

Movement & Episode Settings ?How far the robot turns or drives forward each step, how wide a yaw deadband counts as "roughly straight," how many training episodes to run, and the step limit before an episode times out.

▼

Q-Learning Parameters ?Set the learning rate (how much each new experience updates the Q-table) and the discount factor (how much the agent cares about immediate vs future reward). Lowering gamma makes a distant goal reward decay faster with distance, so it doesn't drown out step-by-step reward differences.

▼

Rewards Configuration ?Set the reward for taking a specific action while the color sensor and yaw-drift together put the robot in a given state. States combine color (Black/Red/Blue tape, Green goal, White) with yaw-drift (Left/Right/Center).
