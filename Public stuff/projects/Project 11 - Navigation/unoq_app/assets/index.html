      Stride calibration

      Pick two landmarks a known distance apart, ideally 40 m or more (measure it on a campus map). Each step miscounted on a short walk throws the stride off by several percent. Stand at the first, press Start, walk normally to the second, press Stop.

      Phone setup

      • Set screen auto-lock to Never while walking (the page can't keep the screen on over http).
      • Turn the ringer/silent switch off and the volume up: cues are spoken.
      • If the page disconnects mid-walk, the board keeps recording. Taps are queued and resent.
